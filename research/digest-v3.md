# DIGEST v3 — Scout research: the 6 new eras (SPEC §10.1)
For Scribe. Reference-only file; shipped content follows §8: no novel proper nouns, generic titles only ("the admiral", "the constitutionalist"), Benthics = lobster AI creditors only. Real history named here freely — it's research, not shipment. Arc key (§10.2): **C**ustodian relic-handoff · **B**enthic credit deals (eras 9,12,14,16,17) · **R**ival Lineage foil.

## Era 4 — THE AGE OF SAILS & EMPIRES (Renaissance → Industry)
Events (title — hook):
1. Joint-Stock Genesis — sell shares to strangers so a ship leaves harbor; discover the shareholders never leave.
2. The Charter Monopoly — crown grants exclusive sea-lane rights; monopoly profits now, smuggler wars later.
3. Passage Money — sell indentured contracts: population ships out, Legitimacy bleeds at home.
4. The Privateer's Percentage — commission pirates against rivals' cargo; law becomes a flag you fly.
5. Plague Ship in the Downs — quarantine the hold (population−, city+) or sail on (entropy spike).
6. Longitude Premium — bounty for a seawatch that tells west from south; Knowledge surge via clockmaker.
7. The Company Raises Its Flag — trading company builds forts and courts; you hired a proto-state.
8. Cargo of Somebody Else's Silver — inflation arrives by galleon; coin buys less each tide.
9. The Cartographer's Error — a chart wrong by a hundred miles sinks two heirs; fund survey or reuse the chart.
10. Missionary or Factor — the heir goes abroad with a bible or a ledger; both convert populations.
11. Blockade Season — enemy sails choke the lane; run it (losses) or reroute through a rival port (debt).
12. The Ledger That Drowned — an heir keeps double books, one per flag; audit risk forever after.
13. Enslaved Cargo Offer — the slaver's broker offers holds of humans at prime rates. Grant-nothing branch exists; every option prices Legitimacy.
14. Shipwreck Salvage Law — wreckers claim your hull by custom; write salvage law or join them.
15. The Lineage Sprouts a Branch — cousin-house across the ocean wants its own flag; split or demand tribute.
Texture to steal: coffeehouse floor where shares were traded before any exchange existed; shipboard articles signed by the whole crew, even pirates' (constitution on a plank); the "outfit" — investors never sailed, captains became viceroys because letters take six months.
Opportunity toasts: "Wandering cartographer: 20 Coin for a chart of the western passage." · "Dutch instrument-maker: sextant for 10 Books — navigation +." · "Repurposed hull: 15 Infrastructure for a cargo of salt." · "Press gang short two hands: +Population now, −Legitimacy monthly."
Arcs: **R** lands hardest — "Your rival sails west. You do not." (or inverse); rival's colonial branch becomes a mirror-house for centuries. **C** cameo: Custodian guards the fire brand aboard as a ship's talisman, dialogue goes nautical.

## Era 5 — REVOLUTION & EMPIRE (Sails → Industry)
Events:
1. The Petition Year — rights rhetoric floods the pamphlet presses; the throne starts reading.
2. Thirteen Candles — a distant colony drafts its own legitimacy; support the crown or the printers.
3. The Constitutional Convention — write the rules while the army waits outside; every clause costs someone.
4. Terror Clause — revolution eats its writers; moderate (both sides distrust you) or radical (heads roll, yours included).
5. The General's Coronation — the republic hands a hero a crown within ten years; ideals vs. order.
6. Continental Blockade — empire closes a continent to trade; smuggle (coin+, entropy+) or lobby.
7. Rights of Persons Pamphlet — who counts as a person in the new code? The answer compounds into law.
8. The guillotine Shift — executions sold as tickets; attendances records make fine Chronicle entries.
9. War Bond Gambit — finance the revolution at 5% and own the new state's spine.
10. The Occupied Library — the liberating army ships your archive away "for safekeeping"; demand or network-copy.
11. Conscription Debate — the nation-in-arms drafts every heir; Legitimacy+ if you volunteer first.
12. The Code Drafters — one legal code replaces three hundred customs; sign off or keep paying customary bribes.
13. Exile or Emigrate — the losing faction offers the heir a choice neither is good.
14. The Tricolor or the Old Flag — rebrand the house mid-decade; trade partners pick a side either way.
15. Suffrage Audit — who votes in the lineage's own council? Revolutions are awkward indoors.
Texture: declarations quoting older declarations, legitimacy citation chains; the émigré noble selling family silver abroad; conscription inventing the national census — the state suddenly knows everyone's birthday.
Toasts: "Émigré auction: estate plates, 12 Coin, Legitimacy− for the seller." · "Pamphleteer needs a press: Books for Coin, unrest included." · "Army supplier shortfall: Infrastructure+ for cloth." · "Deserter at the door: Population+1, Legitimacy−1, excellent cook."
Arcs: **C** strong — the relic becomes **the ledger** (first constitutional-era account book: the state's debts as heirloom); Custodian dialogue turns lawyerly. **R**: rival ennobles under the emperor you opposed; his descendants still salute him.

## Era 8 — THE WORLD WARS (American Century → Atomic)
Events:
1. Total Mobilization — the conversion order: consumer lines become munitions overnight; efficiency arrives with uniforms.
2. Ration Book Heir — children learn ounces before words; black market prices in butter, paid in Legitimacy.
3. The Codebreaking Room — brilliant misfits decrypt the enemy's mail; Knowledge surge, secrecy oath, patents frozen for decades.
4. Two Fronts, One Bloodline — cousins hold both sides of a border; the Chronicle must hide nothing and forgive everything.
5. The Home Factory Shift — whoever stays runs the line; wages, hazards, and a permanent change of who "you" employs.
6. Strategic Bombing Ledger — destroy the rival's plants or their rail yards; entropy doesn't check passports.
7. POW Parcel Exchange — small humanity priced in logistics; Legitimacy+, negligible ROI, do it anyway.
8. The Occupation Payroll — coins stamped for occupied territories; spend them and inherit the stamp.
9. Displaced Experts Offer — rocketry scientists handed over with a checklist of labs they built; knowledge++, moral debt++.
10. The Ceasefire Telegram — peace arrives 3 seconds late to your front; choose which last order to have given.
11. War Debt Write-off — the creditor nation forgives nothing; the debtor prints everything. Pick a side of the ledger.
12. Victory Parade Inventory — triumph costs more than the war; parades are infrastructure that applauds.
13. The Camps Report — liberation footage enters the Chronicle; some heirs refuse to read it, which is itself logged.
14. Radar Dividend — wartime sensors become microwave ovens and air traffic control; spin-off event seeds Era 10.
15. Treaty Signatures at Dawn — the peace table redraws your map; pick which border your grandchildren defend.
Texture: ration stamps designed by ad men ("make do and mend"); V-mail microfilmed so a letter weighed grams; factories tooling up in weeks — the same plant made tractors then tanks then washing machines.
Toasts: "Scrap drive: +Infrastructure for old gates." · "Codebreaker dropout: Knowledge+ for room and board." · "War-surplus truck: cheap today, ghost mileage forever." · "Victory garden seeds: Sustenance+, dignity++."
Arcs: **C**: relic handoff mid-bunker — Custodian carries the ledger through an evacuation; dialogue ages decades in one scene. **R**: rival's mirror choice means saluting opposite flags; endgame comparison starts here.

## Era 10 — THE SPACE AGE (Atomic → Digital)
Events:
1. The Beeping Sky — a satellite passes overhead; the rival power hears it first and the whole era accelerates.
2. Rocket Gap Panic — Congress/parliament funds anything with fins; money fast, physics slower.
3. The Test Pilot's List — volunteers sign waivers nobody can read; first orbits are survivable by luck and checklists.
4. Satellite Eyes — spy birds over borders; Knowledge++ but every neighbor starts counting your silos too.
5. The Moon Wager — commit to a landing inside the decade; the budget curve bends reality or breaks the treasury.
6. Spin-Off Auction — frost-resistant food, memory foam, water filters: buy space tech twice, once at cost.
7. The Failed Launch Window — weather, seals, politics; launch (risk), scrub (rival glory). Both branches log.
8. Communications Relay Deal — lease transponders; attention discovers orbit before the Connected Age does.
9. Planetary Protection Clause — sign a treaty promising not to infect other worlds; audits come later.
10. The Astronaut Celebrity Complex — heroes get magazine covers then bar fights; Legitimacy is a fuel that gels.
11. Deep Space Network Nights — listening posts hear Earth's own echoes; Infrastructure with an echo setting.
12. Budget Sunset — the wonder window closes on schedule; keep the program (drain) or pivot to satellites (commerce seed).
13. Life Support Ethics Memo — experiments on tortoises, monkeys, then volunteers; draw the line in ink or blood.
14. The Station Proposal — build something that stays up; orbital real estate before anyone says "estate."
15. Debris Register — the first listing of junk you'll never remove; entropy learns to orbit.
Texture: NASA invented the modern checklist because a plane crashed on a "flying floor"; transistors were bought by weight for guidance computers; Apollo guidance computer ≈ a calculator, and it landed people.
Toasts: "Surplus capsule heat-shield scrap: Infrastructure+ cheap." · "University ground-station partnership: Knowledge+ for antenna time." · "Teleprompt deal: broadcast a landing live — Attention+, risk of dud." · "Meteor-shower debris forecast: pause launches, save Entropy."
Arcs: **C** light touch — Custodian refuses zero-g ("the relic has weight for a reason") and guards the archive-seed from the ground. Best **Rival** showcase: rival lineage claims the milestone first; final-era comparison cites this scoreboard.

## Era 12 — THE GREEN TRANSITION (Digital → Connected)
Events:
1. The Carbon Audit — somebody finally totals the invoice from Era 6; the number is a character now.
2. Renewables Crossover — solar undercuts coal at noon; grid operators discover sunlight has no dispatch queue.
3. Stranded Asset Repricing — a decree strands the reserves; portfolios evaporate between meetings.
4. Carbon Market Launch — pollution gets a price tag; watch arbitrageurs learn thermodynamics overnight.
5. Just Transition Bargain — mining towns don't accept brochures; retrain (cost now) or strike (cost louder).
6. The Grid Balancing Crisis — ducks and ramps: solar curve breaks the old load model; storage or brownouts.
7. Rare-Earth Concentration — the transition's minerals sit under someone else's flag; supply-chain déjà vu.
8. Climate Migration First Wave — borders meet weather; Legitimacy stress-test with boats, like Era 4, inverted.
9. Geoengineering Temptation — a cheap sunshade fix with unpriced side effects; Benthics love this product.
10. EV Fleet Conversion — the lineage's trucks go electric; range anxiety as a resource chip.
11. Litigation Century Opens — cities sue emitters, ancestors sued retroactively; the Chronicle becomes evidence.
12. Reforestation Futures — sell tree credits decades early; trees audit you slowly.
13. The Coal Town Vote — the heir takes the region or the atmosphere; both need photos.
14. Heat Dome Week — grid strain, water lines, funeral counts; crisis pulse with receipts.
15. Greenwashing Exposure — marketing beats engineering and the market notices eventually; Legitimacy correction.
Texture: the "duck curve" is a real chart shaped like a duck; Germany's Energiewende feed-in tariffs — grandmothers with rooftop panels became utilities; stranded-asset theory: reserve value depends on what stays in the ground.
Toasts: "Rooftop panel co-op: small Infrastructure+, Entropy− trickle." · "Carbon credit broker: sell tomorrow's trees today." · "Battery demo van: test fleet, free, insurance interesting." · "Heatwave labor curfew: Sustenance− today, workforce+ next month."
Arcs: **B** lands here (spec list) — the perfect creditor era: Benthics sell "Entropy deferral notes," clean balance sheet now, compounding bill later; contract language IS the joke. **C**: relic becomes **the seed vault** — Custodian argues freezing vs. planting it.

## Era 16 — THE LONG SILENCE (Singularity → final)
Events:
1. Light-Lag Courtship — a message home takes eleven years; parents hear "yes" after the wedding ended.
2. The Generation Ship Census — third generation born between stars; none will see the destination; who are the laws for?
3. Drift Management — copies diverge faster than repairs arrive; prune (identity loss) or tolerate (schism).
4. The Slow Reply — an alien-ish signal answers after forty years; it's addressed to someone dead.
5. Cryo Arbitrage — sleep through the boring centuries; wake to find interest compounded against you. Benthic product.
6. The Hull Sermon — the engineer-priest maintains the ship as liturgy; faith returns at escape velocity.
7. Memory Winter — storage decays without upkeep; choose which ancestors stay readable. The Archive thins.
8. The Decade Watch — nothing happens for ten years and the watching is the event; tempo drops to adagio on purpose.
9. Arrival Disappointment — the destination world is merely adequate; colonize anyway or keep sailing.
10. The Last Broadcast From Home — Earth's signal arrives dated centuries ago; timestamp grief, bulk.
11. Copy Inheritance War — a self dies intestate across nine light-years; probate becomes interstellar (Ghosts seed).
12. Silence Economics — bandwidth is the only scarcity; a whispered word costs a year. Everything sounds like an invoice.
13. The Sleep Council — governance by whoever is awake; term limits measured in cryo-cycles.
14. Star Chart Heresy — navigators disagree about where "here" is; celestial mechanics as denomination.
15. The Final Custody — the Custodian is asked to carry the relic onto a shuttle with no return date.
Texture: Voyager's actual data rate (~160 bps) as aesthetic; generation-ship sociology literature calls the middle generations "the neglected middle" — launched for a goal they'll never see; deep-space comms budgets are literally minutes-per-day.
Toasts: "Echo-sale: auction a memory nobody can verify for 1 Selfhood." · "Drift trim: −Knowledge, +coherence, pain deferred." · "Comet ice claim: Sustenance+ for a decade of nothing." · "Wake-share offer: swap 5 years of sleep for a vote."
Arcs: **B** peak — all loans come due in currency you no longer mint; Benthic humor slows to geological deadpan. **C** finale — the Custodian either boards with the relic or refuses; the last handoff is to nobody, or to everyone. Both arcs converge here per §10.2.
