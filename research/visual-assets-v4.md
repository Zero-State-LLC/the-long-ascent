# THE LONG ASCENT — Visual Assets v4

Redrew 18 costume sheets (4 frames each), 35 event scene sheets (8 frames each), and all 22 far/near background pairs (44 files). Sheet dimensions and frame counts are unchanged: costumes 512×256; scenes 1280×180; backgrounds 640×180 with two seamless 320×180 tiles. Special/ending scene sheets not referenced by events were preserved. Palette mapping follows `src/render/palettes.js`; no text in art.
