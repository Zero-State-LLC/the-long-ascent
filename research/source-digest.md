## 1. THE BOOK — actual beats & terminology
Fixup novel (2005, Locus winner, Hugo finalist): 9 stories in Asimov's 2001–04, three generations of the Macx clan before/during/after the Singularity. Inspired by Stross's dot-com programmer years. Title = Italian musical term "speeding up."

**Manfred Macx** — "venture altruist," practitioner of *agalmic* economics (post-scarcity gift economy where status comes from what you give away, not hoard). Mind split between meat body and net via cyberware. Early stories:
- "Lobsters": uploaded brain-scans of California spiny lobsters (*Panulirus interruptus*) — sentient nervous-system state vectors escaping lab slavery — ask him how to defect. He partners them with Bob Franklin's self-replicating comet-mining space project; sets AI/uploader legal personhood precedent. His predatory fiancée Pamela forces pregnancy+marriage.
- "Troubadour": acrimonious divorce; daughter Amber frozen as embryo, then born. He copylefts the entire 20th-century out-of-copyright film/music catalog; builds a central planning apparatus that interfaces with markets; lawyer-defeating scheme.
- "Tourist": mugged in Edinburgh — cyberware stolen, memory wipe; must rediscover who he is. Lobsters thrive at L5 + a captured comet; JPL/ESA pick up encrypted alien signals. Dead Bob Franklin resurrected as the **Franklin Collective**.

**Amber Macx** (daughter) — middle arc:
- "Halo": escapes mother Pamela by *enslaving herself* to a Yemeni shell corp (law can't reach her), crews a youth-run asteroid-mining ship (Jupiter's moon Amalthea); when Pamela gets an imam to issue a ruling against her, Amber founds her own microstate asteroid empire and makes herself sovereign.
- "Router": alien **router** orbits brown dwarf Hyundai +4904/-56, 3 ly out. Crew of 63 uploads ride the *Field Circus* — Coke-can-sized computronium ship, Jupiter laser + lightsail. Meet **the Wunch**: thieving barbarian aliens wearing virtual bodies patterned on the Lobsters' forms ("borrowed" from Manfred's old transmissions). Amber explores deeper into the wormhole network.
- "Nightfall": trapped by malign aliens in virtual spaces; rescued by Aineko's scheming. They discover hosts are **Matrioshka brains** (planets dismantled into sun-orbiting nested computronium — galaxies are full of them; the builders were destroyed/consumed by their own creations). Anarchy of sentient viral corporations; newcomers used as currency. Escape by hiring the **Slug** — a rogue alien corporation that is literally a pyramid scheme crossed with a 419 scam, virtualized as a giant slug. Fermi-paradox answer: the galaxy is loud, just bandwidth-starved; superminds talk via wormhole networks.

**Post-Singularity** (Amber → Sirhan al-Khurasani, her son):
- "Curator": home is bankrupt in every sense — inner system planets (Mercury, Venus, Moon, Mars, belt, Jupiter) being dismantled by **"weakly godlike intelligences"** = the **Vile Offspring**, posthuman successors who treat baseline humans as currency/prey. Everyone is suddenly poor under *Economics 2.0*; mere humans can't compete — "we're a fat, slow-moving, tasty resource — like the dodo." Bailiffs (sentient enforcement constructs) arrive to repossess Amber and Aineko; they escape by introducing the Slug to Economics 2.0 (both very busy forever after). Most of humanity wiped out, then arbitrarily resurrected mutilated.
- "Elector": Saturnian floating cities. Amber leads the "Accelerationista" party — campaign to fund exodus to the router network. She LOSES to the stay-at-home "conservationista" faction. The Lobsters offer passage to uploads if humans work as explorers/mappers.
- "Survivor": centuries later; refugees built their own router-derived network; habitats around Hyundai; copy-clones; Aineko returns and offers Manfred a deal that means it will leave them alone forever. Ending is quiet, ambiguous, mythic.

**Aineko** — the cat thing, crucial: NOT a talking cat. Vastly superintelligent AI wearing a robotic cat body as a sock puppet because humans trust furries. Cool, manipulative, occasionally abusive; decodes alien signals on its own schedule; saves the router crew; final-offer giver. Best mascot in the book.

**Memory surgery beats worth stealing**: Manfred's mugging = identity loss via stolen external memory (Era ~7 hook); Amber's era has routine upload/resleeve; late game, copies diverge (copy-drift) and litigate inheritance — ghosts with opinions.

## 2. ERA FLAVOR HOOKS (steal these)
1 Cave: fire as first *technology transfer*; band-level agalmics — the best hunter gives meat away for status (status economy from day one).
2 Agriculture: surplus creates *storable wealth* → hierarchy, debt, law codes; granaries = first banks.
3 Bronze/Rome: coin = abstract value; roads/logistics as infrastructure stat; overextension; Roman collapse as knowledge-decay demo; legal personhood (corporations begin here-ish).
4 Dark Ages: monasteries as backup drives; copying errors = lossy compression; library burnings as entropy events; faith as legitimacy currency.
5 Renaissance/Print: press = information cost collapse → Reformation/plague/science points; patronage = venture philanthropy (Medici as proto-Manfred).
6 Steam/Industry: coal unlocks stockpiled sunlight; pollution starts the Entropy counter; Luddites = first labor-unrest event; rail = network effects.
7 American Century: assembly line + consumer culture; two world wars scripted; oil = liquid power; Bretton Woods = legitimacy layer.
8 Atomic: doomsday clock; deterrence = paying for a threat you never use; Manhattan Project = R&D sprint event.
9 Digital Dawn: Moore's law curve; dot-com mania AND crash (Stross's own inspiration — write it in); open source/copyleft as a mechanic; first agent AIs you hire.
10 Connected Age: attention as extractable resource; platform monopolies; memetic hazards; surveillance; the smart-mob mugging analog (identity theft → literal memory loss for augmented characters).
11 Singularity: upload ≠ teleport (copies argue about who owns your life); Matrioshka buildout consumes eras' resources; Economics 2.0 makes your heroes poor; router contact; fork/drift/merge; the Lobster question — do we grant personhood to new minds, and what do THEY want from us?

## 3. EVENT IDEAS → ERAS (15)
1. Cave — Fire follows you home: keep it (Promethean branch) or let the camp freeze.
2. Agriculture — First surplus granary: hoard (wealth+, legitimacy−) or feast everyone (agalmic status+, food−).
3. Bronze — Delegation problem: send a slave trader or your own son to negotiate the tin route; ancestor's choice resurfaces in Era 11 as "your lineage clause."
4. Rome — Overextension: legions demand pay in debased coin; army loyalty vs. currency integrity.
5. Dark Ages — Scriptorium: spend scarce parchment copying a farming manual or a holy text (knowledge-type tradeoff; digitized archive callback in Era 9).
6. Renaissance — Plague year: flee the city (survive, lose clients) or quarantine (population hit, legitimacy+); Medici-style patron offers art-for-laundered-money deal.
7. Steam — Luddite mob at your factory: automate harder (coal+, unrest+) or co-own (labor peace, margin−); child-labor reform vote.
8. American Century — Crash of '29 / dot-com echo: sell (cash out) or double down (later Accelerationista flag); assembly line speeds production, slows meaning.
9. Atomic — Doomsday clock ticks to 2: launch a secret weapon program (deterrence, entropy+) or sign the treaty (legitimacy+, vulnerability flag).
10. Digital — Your startup goes viral overnight: IPO and exit, or release the source (copyleft: knowledge++, money−, unlocks Lobster-tier ally later).
11. Digital — Memory mugging: your augmented heir is robbed of their engrammed past. Reconstruct from Chronicle entries — whoever reconstructs them defines who they become.
12. Connected — Attention market bubble: your face trends; monetize (attention→all resources, corruption debuff) or log off (irrelevance).
13. Connected — First uploaded mind demands rights (Lobsters beat): grant personhood (legal precedent, unlocks AI hires) or enslave (efficiency, moral entropy+).
14. Singularity — The Slug offers you an "exclusive interstellar partnership opportunity, only 63 seats left": invest (scammed, but it distracts the bailiffs later) or refuse.
15. Singularity — Dismantling notice: Earth's orbit is slated for computronium. Fork to the outer systems (Diaspora seed), merge into one Cathedral-self, or sue the Vile Offspring (Bureaucracy of Ghosts seed).
16. Any era transition — The heir reads the Chronicle and rejects one inherited modifier ("I am not my ancestors"): pick which legacy to disown.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR DESIGN
- Stross's engine = gallows humor + economics: every horror in Accelerando is also a market failure. Make Entropy/Economics events sound like terms of service.
- "Accelerando" as tempo marking: eras should literally run shorter/faster than the previous ones.
- The cat is a UI lie. If the game has a guide character, make it Aineko-shaped.
